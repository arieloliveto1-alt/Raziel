<h3>Ruta &amp; Combustible AR</h3>

<p>
A fuel planning and travel cost calculator designed specifically for the Argentine market.
</p>

<h4>Main Features</h4>

<ul>
<li>Extensive vehicle database covering historical and current models.</li>
<li>Fuel consumption estimates based on public and technical sources.</li>
<li>Travel cost estimation.</li>
<li>Fuel requirement calculation.</li>
<li>Vehicle autonomy estimation.</li>
<li>Driving modes: City, Highway, and Mixed.</li>
<li>Support for Gasoline, Diesel, and Hybrid vehicles.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Purpose</h4>

<p>
Ruta &amp; Combustible AR helps drivers estimate fuel usage, travel costs, and vehicle range
before starting a trip, providing useful planning information through a simple and intuitive interface.
