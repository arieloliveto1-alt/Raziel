Products

Ruta & Combustible AR

A fuel planning and travel cost calculator designed specifically for the Argentine market.

Main Features

  • Extensive vehicle database covering historical and current models.
  • Fuel consumption estimates based on public and technical sources.
  • Travel cost estimation.
  • Fuel requirement calculation.
  • Vehicle autonomy estimation.
  • Driving modes: City, Highway, and Mixed.
  • Support for Gasoline, Diesel, and Hybrid vehicles.

Purpose

Ruta & Combustible AR helps drivers estimate fuel usage, travel costs, and vehicle range before starting a trip, providing useful planning information through a simple and intuitive interface.

PrayRosary

PrayRosary is a multilingual Catholic prayer application designed to help people around the world pray the Holy Rosary with simplicity, beauty, and devotion.

Main Features

  • Complete Holy Rosary with guided prayers.
  • Support for 20 international languages.
  • High-quality audio prayers.
  • Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious, and Luminous Mysteries.
  • Illustrated mysteries with inspirational artwork.
  • Interactive rosary beads for easy prayer progression.
  • Elegant, intuitive, and distraction-free interface.
  • Designed for daily prayer, meditation, and spiritual reflection.

Purpose

PrayRosary provides Catholics and anyone interested in Christian spirituality with a beautiful digital companion for praying the Holy Rosary anywhere, anytime. By offering multilingual support and guided audio, the application makes one of the world's oldest Christian devotions accessible to users from different cultures and languages.
